# MixMind User Workflow

MixMind is designed to make organizing a large music library easier by combining AI automation with user control. The overall process takes users from adding their music to discovering songs they may have forgotten about.

## 1. Add or Scan Songs

The process starts when users **add new songs or scan their existing music library**. MixMind can work with a large collection, making it possible to organize hundreds or thousands of tracks instead of requiring users to sort everything manually.

## 2. AI Analyzes the Music

After songs are added, MixMind analyzes the available information and audio characteristics of each track. The AI can suggest metadata such as **artist, genre, mood, BPM, year, album, and other useful information**.

For example, MixMind might identify a song as an energetic house track with a BPM of 125.

## 3. Review AI Suggestions

Instead of automatically changing the entire library, MixMind gives users an opportunity to **review the AI's suggestions**. Users can see what the AI thinks each song should be labeled or categorized as.

Confidence scores could also help users identify suggestions that may need more attention.

## 4. Edit or Approve

Users can **approve, reject, or edit** the AI's suggestions. If MixMind correctly identifies a song, the user can approve it. If something is incorrect, the user can change the information before it is added to the organized library.

Users could also approve multiple similar suggestions at once through **batch approval**, which can save time when working with a large collection.

## 5. Organized Library

Once suggestions are approved, MixMind organizes the songs using their metadata. Songs can be easier to group and filter by **genre, mood, BPM, artist, year, album, or other categories**.

The result is a music library that is easier to manage without requiring the user to manually organize every track.

## 6. Search and Discover Music

After the library is organized, users can quickly **search and discover music**. They can find songs based on what they need at the moment.

For example, a DJ could search for energetic house songs around 125 BPM for an upcoming set. A regular listener could search for older chill songs they have not listened to in a while.

## The Complete MixMind Experience

**Add/Scan Songs → AI Analyzes Music → Review Suggestions → Edit/Approve → Organized Library → Search/Discover Music**

The goal of this workflow is to combine the **speed of AI with human decision-making**. MixMind handles repetitive organization tasks, while the user stays in control of how their music library is ultimately organized.
