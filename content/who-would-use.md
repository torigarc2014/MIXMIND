
The main people who would use **MixMind** are **DJs and people with large music libraries** who want an easier way to organize and find their songs.

### Main Users

**1. DJs**

- Have hundreds or thousands of songs
- Need to quickly find songs for sets
- Organize music by genre, mood, BPM, or energy
- Spend a lot of time manually organizing their library

**2. Beginner DJs**

- May not know the best way to organize a music collection
- Can use MixMind's AI suggestions to get started
- Can learn how different organization methods work while still controlling the final results

**3. Music Enthusiasts**

- Have large personal collections
- Want to clean up messy or inconsistent music folders
- Want an easier way to search through their music

**4. Producers and Musicians**

- May have large collections of samples, tracks, and reference music
- Could use organization by genre, mood, BPM, or year to find music faster
