
### How MixMind makes things easier

- **Automatic organization:** MixMind uses AI to suggest categories for songs instead of making the user organize everything manually.
- **Less time spent sorting:** Users don't have to go through hundreds or thousands of songs one at a time.
- **Easy searching:** Users can quickly find songs based on **genre, mood, BPM, artist, or year**.
- **Simple review process:** Users can look at MixMind's suggestions and **approve, edit, or reject** them.
- **Better music discovery:** Organized categories can help users find songs they forgot they had.
- **Easier DJ preparation:** DJs can quickly find songs that match the vibe or BPM they need for a set.
- **Less overwhelming:** Instead of looking through a huge, messy library, users have a cleaner and more organized collection.
