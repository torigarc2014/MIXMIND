## BPM

### ### What is BPM?

**BPM stands for Beats Per Minute.** It measures how fast a song's beat is. In simple terms, it tells you how many beats happen in one minute.

### How does BPM work?

For example:

- **60 BPM** = 60 beats per minute → slow
- **100 BPM** = 100 beats per minute → medium
- **128 BPM** = 128 beats per minute → fast
- **150 BPM** = 150 beats per minute → very fast

Music software can analyze a song and estimate its BPM by detecting the regular rhythm or beat pattern.

### What is BPM used for?

BPM is especially useful for **DJs and music organization**. DJs can use BPM to find songs with similar speeds and create smoother transitions between tracks.

For example, if a DJ is playing a song at **126 BPM**, they might look for another song around **124–128 BPM** so the transition feels natural.

### How BPM helps MixMind?

For your **MixMind AI Music Library Organizer**, BPM could be used as a searchable piece of metadata. A user could search:

 **House + 125–130 BPM + High Energy**

MixMind could then show songs that match those characteristics.

So, basically: **BPM tells you how fast a song is, and MixMind can use that information to help users organize and find the right music faster.**
