## What Is BPM?

BPM stands for Beats Per Minute, and it is used to measure the tempo, or speed, of a song. It tells you how many beats happen in one minute. A song with a lower BPM usually sounds slower and more relaxed, while a song with a higher BPM will sound faster and more energetic. For example, a slow song might be around 60–80 BPM, while many house and EDM songs are around 120–130 BPM. BPM is especially useful for DJs because it helps them match songs together when mixing. If two songs have similar BPMs, it can be easier to transition between them smoothly without making the music sound too fast or too slow. BPM can also be useful in an AI music library because it allows users to search and organize songs based on tempo. This makes it easier to find the right song for a workout, party, or DJ set.
