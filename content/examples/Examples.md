## How MixMind will help?

### Examples of How MixMind Will Help

- **Organize large music libraries:** MixMind can automatically organize hundreds or thousands of songs instead of making the user sort them manually.
- **Add music metadata:** It can identify information such as **artist, genre, BPM, year, and mood**.
- **Find songs faster:** A DJ could search for something like _“house songs around 125 BPM”_ and quickly find matching tracks.
- **Create better playlists:** MixMind could group songs by mood, genre, BPM, or activity, such as workout, party, relaxing, or DJ sets.
- **Discover forgotten songs:** It can identify older or rarely played songs in a user's library that might fit their current preferences.
- **Help DJs prepare sets:** A DJ could search for songs that match the BPM and genre of the current track, making it easier to build a smooth set.
- **Fix inconsistent organization:** If songs have messy file names or are stored in different folders, MixMind can help standardize their information.
- **Keep the user in control:** Instead of automatically changing everything, MixMind can recommend how songs should be organized while allowing the user to accept, reject, or edit the suggestions.

**Example:** A DJ has 2,000 songs but needs a **125–128 BPM house track with an energetic mood**. Instead of searching through folders one by one, MixMind could filter the library and show the songs that match those characteristics.
