# MixMind User Control

MixMind is designed to help users organize their music without completely taking control away from them. When MixMind analyzes a song, it can suggest information such as the genre, mood, BPM, or other categories. Instead of automatically making every decision, users can **review the AI suggestions** before they are added to their library.

Users can **approve** a suggestion if it looks correct, **reject** it if the AI got something wrong, or **edit** the information themselves. For example, MixMind might label a song as “Chill,” but a user may feel that it fits better under “Energetic.” The user can change the suggestion and decide how they want the song organized.

Keeping the user in control is important because everyone organizes their music differently. A DJ might have their own genres, moods, playlists, or naming system that makes sense to them. AI can make organizing thousands of songs much faster, but it may not always understand the user's personal preferences.

The goal of MixMind is for **AI to assist rather than completely take over**. MixMind does the time-consuming work of analyzing and suggesting organization, while the user makes the final decisions. This creates a balance where AI makes music organization easier while users still have control over their own music library.
