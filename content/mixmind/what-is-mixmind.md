## What is MIXMIND?

**MixMind** is an AI-powered music library organizer designed for DJs and music lovers with large collections of songs. It can analyze music and automatically organize tracks using information such as **artist, genre, BPM, mood, and release year**. Instead of manually sorting hundreds or thousands of songs into folders and playlists, users can use MixMind to quickly search, filter, and discover music. For DJs, MixMind could make it easier to find songs that match the tempo or mood of a set. It could also help users rediscover songs they forgot they had.
