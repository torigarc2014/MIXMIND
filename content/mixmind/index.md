## Summary

**MixMind** is an AI-powered music library organizer designed to help DJs and music lovers manage large collections of songs. Instead of manually organizing hundreds or thousands of tracks, MixMind uses AI to identify and organize important information such as **artist, genre, mood, BPM, and release year**. Users can search and filter their music based on these details to quickly find the right song.

For DJs, MixMind can make preparing playlists and sets easier by finding songs with similar BPMs, genres, or moods. It can also help users discover older songs they may have forgotten about. Most importantly, MixMind keeps the user in control by allowing them to review, change, or approve the AI's suggestions. The goal is to make music organization **faster, easier, and more personalized**.
