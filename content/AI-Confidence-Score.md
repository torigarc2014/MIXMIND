# MixMind AI Confidence Scores

MixMind could show users a **confidence score** to explain how certain the AI is about the information it assigns to a song. For example, MixMind might be 95% confident that a song is house music, but only 62% confident about its mood. This gives users a better idea of which AI suggestions are more certain and which ones may need another look.

Confidence scores can be especially helpful when MixMind is organizing a large music library. Users do not necessarily need to check every song equally. A song with a high confidence score might only need a quick review, while a song with a low confidence score could be flagged for the user to double-check. For example, if MixMind is unsure whether a track is **deep house or tech house**, the user can listen to it and choose the category that makes the most sense.

This feature also supports **transparency and human-centered AI**. Instead of making AI decisions appear completely certain, MixMind gives users information about the AI's level of confidence. This helps users understand when the system may be uncertain and encourages them to stay involved in the organization process.

The goal is not for users to blindly trust the AI. **MixMind should make suggestions, show how confident those suggestions are, and let the user make the final decision.** This creates a better balance between AI automation and human control.
