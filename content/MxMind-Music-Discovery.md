# MixMind Music Discovery

MixMind is not only useful for organizing a music library; it can also help users **rediscover songs they may have forgotten about**. When someone has hundreds or thousands of songs, it can be easy for older tracks to get buried and rarely played. By organizing songs with useful information like mood, genre, BPM, and year, MixMind makes it easier to find music that fits what the user is looking for.

For example, a user could search for **energetic house songs** when they want something upbeat. They could also look for songs around **125 BPM** for a DJ set or search for music from a specific **year or genre**. This could bring older songs back to the user's attention that they may have forgotten were even in their library.

This can be especially helpful for **DJs preparing for sets**. Instead of scrolling through thousands of songs, a DJ could use MixMind to find tracks based on the energy, genre, BPM, or time period they want. For example, a DJ getting ready for a late-night house set could look for high-energy house tracks between 120–128 BPM. MixMind could help narrow down the library and make it easier to discover songs that fit the set.

Overall, MixMind turns music organization into a way to **discover and rediscover music**. The better the library is organized, the easier it becomes to find songs that match the user's current mood, activity, or DJ set.
