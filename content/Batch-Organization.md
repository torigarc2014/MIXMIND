# MixMind Batch Organization

MixMind is designed to help users organize **large music libraries**, including collections with hundreds or even thousands of songs. Organizing that many songs one at a time can take a lot of time, especially for DJs who are constantly adding new music. MixMind can analyze multiple songs at once and make organization suggestions based on information such as genre, mood, BPM, artist, and year.

There are two ways MixMind could organize music: **individually or in batches**. With individual organization, users review each song separately and approve, reject, or edit the AI's suggestions. This gives the user more control, but it can become slow when dealing with a very large library.

Batch organization allows users to review **multiple songs at the same time**. For example, MixMind could identify 50 songs as energetic house music and allow the user to approve the suggestions together. If the suggestions look correct, the user could approve the entire group instead of reviewing every song individually.

Batch approval could save DJs a significant amount of time. Instead of spending hours sorting thousands of tracks, DJs could quickly review groups of similar songs and make changes only when needed. This keeps the user involved while making the organization process much faster.

The goal is not for MixMind to automatically change everything without permission. **Batch organization gives users a faster way to review AI suggestions while still keeping the final decision in their hands.**
